# Computing and AI

Software design, language model behavior, and the governance of AI systems.

## Software design

[On the Criteria to Be Used in Decomposing Systems into Modules](1971_Parnas_On_the_Criteria_to_Be_Used_in_Decomposing_Systems_into_Modules_CMU_Technical_Report.pdf) (1971), David Parnas, Carnegie Mellon University technical report. Dated August 1971 (CMU-CS-71-101). Argues that the benefit of modularization, in flexibility, comprehensibility, and shorter development time, depends on the criteria used to divide a system, and compares a conventional and an unconventional decomposition for each of two design problems, finding advantages in the unconventional one.

## Language models

[AI Models Collapse When Trained on Recursively Generated Data](2024_Shumailov_AI_Models_Collapse_When_Trained_on_Recursively_Generated_Data.pdf) (2024), Ilia Shumailov and colleagues, Nature. Shows that training on model-generated content causes model collapse, in which the tails of the original data distribution disappear, in language models, variational autoencoders, and Gaussian mixture models.

[Programming Refusal with Conditional Activation Steering](2025_Lee_Programming_Refusal_with_Conditional_Activation_Steering_ICLR.pdf) (2025), Bruce Lee and colleagues, ICLR. Introduces CAST, which reads a model's activation patterns at inference time and applies steering only for chosen categories of input, allowing rules such as refusing hate speech or requests outside legal advice.

[LLMs as Method Actors: A Model for Prompt Engineering and Architecture](2024_Doyle_LLMs_as_Method_Actors_A_Model_for_Prompt_Engineering_and_Architecture.pdf) (2024), Colin Doyle. Treats models as actors, prompts as scripts, and responses as performances, and shows on the Connections word puzzle that this framing improves GPT-4o over vanilla and chain-of-thought prompting.

[An AI System to Help Scientists Write Expert-Level Empirical Software](2026_Aygun_An_AI_System_to_Help_Scientists_Write_Expert-Level_Empirical_Software_Nature.pdf) (2026), Eser Aygun and colleagues, Nature. Presents Empirical Research Assistance (ERA), which combines a large language model with tree search to write scientific software that maximizes a quality metric, with results that include software for forecasting COVID-19 hospitalizations. This copy is the unedited accelerated article preview.

## Risk and governance

[Foundations for Risk Assessment of AI in Protecting Fundamental Rights](2025_Ferrigno_Rotolo_Novelli_Sartor_Foundations_for_Risk_Assessment_of_AI_in_Protecting_Fundamental_Rights.pdf) (2025), Beatrice Ferrigno, Antonino Rotolo, Claudio Novelli, Giovanni Sartor, and colleagues. A conceptual framework for qualitative AI risk assessment under the EU AI Act, combining proportionality-based definitional balancing with defeasible reasoning.

[Leveraging Strategic Foresight to Mitigate Artificial Intelligence Risk in Public Sectors](2025_UN_DESA_Policy_Brief_174_Leveraging_Strategic_Foresight_to_Mitigate_AI_Risk_in_Public_Sectors.pdf) (2025), UN Department of Economic and Social Affairs, Policy Brief No. 174. Argues that strategic foresight and scenario planning can help governments anticipate the consequences of AI as they pursue the Sustainable Development Goals.

## Data fusion

[A Common Operating Picture Framework Leveraging Data Fusion and Deep Learning](2020_Ortiz_A_Common_Operating_Picture_Framework_Leveraging_Data_Fusion_and_Deep_Learning.pdf) (2020), Benjamin Ortiz and colleagues, Accenture Federal Services. A platform that extracts information from separate data sources with deep learning, fuses it, and presents detected and geolocated events in one real-time picture with forensic review.
