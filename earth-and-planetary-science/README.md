# Earth and Planetary Science

Meteorite impact and shock metamorphism, the Gulf Coast geology of Louisiana and Mississippi, geotechnical machine learning, and seismology.

## Recognizing impact structures and shock metamorphism

[The Convincing Identification of Terrestrial Meteorite Impact Structures: What Works, What Doesn't, and Why](2010_French_Koeberl_The_Convincing_Identification_of_Terrestrial_Meteorite_Impact_Structures.pdf) (2010), Bevan French and Christian Koeberl, Earth-Science Reviews 98, pp. 123-170. A 48-page review of the diagnostic evidence for impact origin; only shock-metamorphic effects such as shatter cones and planar deformation features in quartz, and in some cases traces of the meteorite itself, are generally accepted as unambiguous.

[Shock Metamorphism of Quartz in Nature and Experiment: II. Significance in Geoscience](1996_Grieve_Langenhorst_Stoffler_Shock_Metamorphism_of_Quartz_in_Nature_and_Experiment_II_Significance_in_Geoscience.pdf) (1996), Richard Grieve, Falko Langenhorst, and Dieter Stoffler, Meteoritics and Planetary Science 31, pp. 6-35. Compares shock effects in quartz from natural impacts and laboratory experiments, and concludes that the orientation of planar microstructures is the most useful natural shock barometer because post-impact heating does not alter it.

[Deformation and Transformation of Rock-Forming Minerals by Natural and Experimental Shock Processes. I. Behavior of Minerals Under Shock Compression](1972_Stoffler_Deformation_and_Transformation_of_Rock-Forming_Minerals_by_Natural_and_Experimental_Shock_Processes_I_Behavior_of_Minerals_Under_Shock_Compression.pdf) (1972), Dieter Stoffler, Fortschritte der Mineralogie 49, pp. 50-113. A 64-page treatment of how minerals behave under shock compression, with 36 figures and 6 tables, based on the author's habilitation thesis. This is a scan without a text layer.

[Shock Metamorphism](2022_Xie_Osinski_Shock_Metamorphism_Encyclopedia_of_Lunar_Science_Entry.pdf) (2022), Tianqi Xie and Gordon Osinski, Encyclopedia of Lunar Science. A nine-page reference entry defining shock metamorphism and summarizing the irreversible effects, melting, and vaporization that hypervelocity impact produces.

[Raman Study of Shock Features in Plagioclase Feldspar from the Mistastin Lake Impact Structure](2020_Xie_Osinski_Shieh_Raman_Study_of_Shock_Features_in_Plagioclase_Feldspar_Mistastin_Lake_Impact_Structure.pdf) (2020), Tianqi Xie, Gordon Osinski, and Sean Shieh, Meteoritics and Planetary Science 55, pp. 1471-1490. Uses Raman spectroscopy on plagioclase from the Canadian Mistastin Lake structure, finds twins, needle-like inclusions, and planar features but notably no planar deformation features, and cautions that composition and crystal orientation also shift the Raman peaks.

[Shock Metamorphism and Petrography of the Shergotty Achondrite](1986_Stoffler_Ostertag_Jammes_Shock_Metamorphism_and_Petrography_of_the_Shergotty_Achondrite.pdf) (1986), Dieter Stoffler and colleagues, Geochimica et Cosmochimica Acta 50, pp. 889-903. Petrography of a Martian meteorite, with shock effects in its pyroxene, plagioclase, and quartz that indicate a single shock event of about 29 GPa.

## Impact ejecta, glass, and craters

[Impacts into Quartz Sand: Crater Formation, Shock Metamorphism, and Ejecta Distribution](2016_Wunnemann_Zhu_Stoffler_Impacts_into_Quartz_Sand_Crater_Formation_Shock_Metamorphism_and_Ejecta_Distribution.pdf) (2016), Kai Wunnemann, Meng-Hua Zhu, and Dieter Stoffler, Meteoritics and Planetary Science 51, pp. 1762-1794. Vertical-gun cratering experiments in quartz sand compared with iSALE shock physics simulations; ejecta thickness falls off as a power law with distance, and the shocked ejecta divide into melt particles, shock-lithified aggregates, and shock-comminuted grains.

[Determining the Age and Possibility for an Extraterrestrial Impact Formation Mechanism of the Ilumetsa Structures (Estonia)](2020_Losiak_Determining_the_Age_and_Possibility_for_an_Extraterrestrial_Impact_Formation_Mechanism_of_the_Ilumetsa_Structures_Estonia.pdf) (2020), Anna Losiak and colleagues, Meteoritics and Planetary Science 55, pp. 274-293. Radiocarbon dating of charcoal in the putative ejecta shows two small rimmed structures formed together about 7,000 years ago, although unequivocal proof of an impact remains lacking.

[Australasian Microtektites and Associated Impact Ejecta in the South China Sea and the Middle Pleistocene Supereruption of Toba](2006_Glass_Koeberl_Australasian_Microtektites_and_Associated_Impact_Ejecta_in_the_South_China_Sea_and_the_Middle_Pleistocene_Supereruption_of_Toba.pdf) (2006), Billy Glass and Christian Koeberl, Meteoritics and Planetary Science 41, pp. 305-326. Reports the first shock-metamorphosed rock fragments, containing coesite and stishovite, found in the Australasian microtektite layer.

[Geochemistry of Darwin Impact Glass and Target Rocks](1990_Meisel_Koeberl_Ford_Geochemistry_of_Darwin_Impact_Glass_and_Target_Rocks.pdf) (1990), Thomas Meisel, Christian Koeberl, and R. J. Ford, Geochimica et Cosmochimica Acta 54, pp. 1463-1474. Chemical analyses of 18 Darwin glass samples and 7 target rocks, identifying three glass groups and showing that the glass can be produced by melting and mixing local quartzite and shale.

[Rb-Sr Study of Impact Glass and Country Rocks from the Henbury Meteorite Crater Field](1969_Compston_Taylor_Rb-Sr_Study_of_Impact_Glass_and_Country_Rocks_from_the_Henbury_Meteorite_Crater_Field.pdf) (1969), William Compston and S. R. Taylor, Geochimica et Cosmochimica Acta 33, pp. 1037-1043. Rubidium-strontium data showing that no selective loss of rubidium occurred when the country rock melted to glass.

[Exploratory Experiments of Impact Craters Formed in Viscous-Liquid Targets: Analogs for Martian Rampart Craters?](1978_Gault_Greeley_Exploratory_Experiments_of_Impact_Craters_in_Viscous-Liquid_Targets_Analogs_for_Martian_Rampart_Craters.pdf) (1978), Donald Gault and Ronald Greeley, Icarus 34, pp. 486-495. Impacts into soupy mud that support the idea that the ejecta around Martian rampart craters were emplaced as a flow of fluidized material.

[Three-Dimensional Reconstruction of Planar Deformation Features from Single Electron Micrographs](2020_Leon-Cazares_Kienl_Rae_Three-Dimensional_Reconstruction_of_Planar_Deformation_Features_from_Single_Electron_Micrographs.pdf) (2020), F. D. Leon-Cazares, C. Kienl, and C. M. F. Rae, Metallurgical and Materials Transactions A 51. A materials-science method for reconstructing dislocations in three dimensions from one micrograph, demonstrated in a nickel-based superalloy. Its planar deformation features are crystal defects in metal, not the impact features in quartz.

## Gulf Coast geology and landforms

[The Pliocene Citronelle Formation of the Gulf Coastal Plain](1916_Matson_The_Pliocene_Citronelle_Formation_of_the_Gulf_Coastal_Plain_USGS_Professional_Paper_98-L.pdf) (1916), George Charlton Matson, USGS Professional Paper 98-L. A study begun in 1910 to separate the older Lafayette and Grand Gulf formations into better stratigraphic units that could be correlated with fossil-dated formations, covering the later Tertiary deposits from western Florida west to the Sabine River.

[Post-Oligocene Stratigraphy and Mapping Considerations](1995_May_et_al_Post-Oligocene_Stratigraphy_and_Mapping_Considerations.pdf) (1995), James May and colleagues, Mississippi Geology 16(1). Discusses the difficulty of separating and mapping the Catahoula, Hattiesburg, Pascagoula, and Citronelle formations in southern Mississippi, and why it matters for groundwater and landfill siting.

[Geology of the Chipola 7.5-Minute Quadrangle, Louisiana](Louisiana_Geological_Survey_Geology_of_the_Chipola_7.5-Minute_Quadrangle_Louisiana.pdf), Louisiana Geological Survey. Describes the surface geology of a quadrangle in East Feliciana and St. Helena Parishes: Pliocene Citronelle uplands, Pleistocene Montpelier and Prairie terraces along the Amite River, thin loess, and Holocene alluvium. No publication year is given in the document.

[Landforms of the Louisiana Coastal Plain](2019_Snead_McCulloh_Heinrich_Landforms_of_the_Louisiana_Coastal_Plain_LGS_Maps_and_Atlas_Series_No_15.pdf) (2019), John Snead, Richard McCulloh, and Paul Heinrich, Louisiana Geological Survey Maps and Atlas Series No. 15. The 40-page pamphlet accompanying a landform map of the Louisiana Coastal Plain.

## Geotechnics and seismology

[Machine Learning-Based Synthetic CPT Prediction in Offshore Windfarms with Sparse Data](2023_Qu_Machine_Learning-Based_Synthetic_CPT_Prediction_in_Offshore_Windfarms_with_Sparse_Data.pdf) (2023), D. Qu, 4th EAGE Global Energy Transition Conference. Predicts cone penetration test profiles from geophysical data in a German North Sea windfarm; predictions are realistic outside glacial valleys but weaker inside them.

[Buletini Mujor i Sizmologjise (Monthly Bulletin of Seismology), No. 9](2020_03_Buletini_Mujor_i_Sizmologjise_Monthly_Bulletin_of_Seismology_No_9_Polytechnic_University_of_Tirana.pdf) (March 2020), Polytechnic University of Tirana. In Albanian. A monthly catalog of earthquakes recorded, located, and analyzed by the Albanian seismological network, with station data, parameter quality analysis, and epicenter maps.
