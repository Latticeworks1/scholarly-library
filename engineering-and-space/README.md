# Engineering and Space

Spacecraft program history and ground software, geotechnical interpretation, drafting standards, and Defense Intelligence Agency reference documents on emerging technologies.

[The Interplanetary Pioneers, Volume II: System Design and Development](The_Interplanetary_Pioneers_Volume_II_System_Design_and_Development_NASA_SP-279_Corliss_1972.pdf) (1972), William R. Corliss, NASA SP-279. The second of three volumes on Pioneers 6, 7, 8, and 9, the four spacecraft that were the first to orbit the Sun systematically at widely separated points in space; this volume covers how the Pioneer system was defined, designed, and developed. The other volumes are the summary (SP-278) and operations and scientific results (SP-280).

[Settle3 CPT Data Interpretation Theory Manual](Settle3_CPT_Data_Interpretation_Theory_Manual_Rocscience_2025.pdf) (2025), Rocscience. Documents the empirical correlations used to turn cone penetration test readings (tip resistance, sleeve friction, pore pressure) into soil parameters such as unit weight, overburden and pre-consolidation stress, soil behaviour type index, shear wave velocity, equivalent SPT N60, hydraulic conductivity, and friction angle, with notes on their reliability.

[MIL-STD-100G: Department of Defense Standard Practice for Engineering Drawings](Standard_Practice_for_Engineering_Drawings_MIL-STD-100G_DoD_1997.pdf) (June 9, 1997). The 84-page US Department of Defense standard for preparing engineering drawings, superseding MIL-STD-100F of September 1996.

[Lunar Prospector Ground System Software: User's Guide and Programmer's Guide](Lunar_Prospector_Ground_System_Software_Users_and_Programmers_Guide_Lockheed_Martin_1998.pdf) (1998), Lockheed Martin Missiles and Space, document LMMS/P458-3022D, prepared under NASA contract NAS2-14256. The user and programmer guide for the ground software used to command the Lunar Prospector mission.

[Cognitive Limits on Simultaneous Control of Multiple Unmanned Spacecraft](DIRD_Cognitive_Limits_on_Simultaneous_Control_of_Multiple_Unmanned_Spacecraft_2010.pdf) (2010), Defense Intelligence Agency, Defense Intelligence Reference Document DIA-08-1101-001, Defense Futures series, dated 15 December 2010.

[Detection and High Resolution Tracking of Vehicles at Hypersonic Velocities](DIRD_Detection_and_High_Resolution_Tracking_of_Vehicles_at_Hypersonic_Velocities_2010.pdf) (2010), Defense Intelligence Agency, Defense Intelligence Reference Document DIA-08-1012-001, Defense Futures series, dated 20 November 2010.

[High-Frequency Gravitational Wave Communications](DIRD_High-Frequency_Gravitational_Wave_Communications_2010.pdf) (2010), Defense Intelligence Agency, Defense Intelligence Reference Document DIA-08-1004-005, Acquisition Threat Support series, dated 6 April 2010.

[Metamaterials for Aerospace Applications](DIRD_Metamaterials_for_Aerospace_Applications_2010.pdf) (2010), Defense Intelligence Agency, Defense Intelligence Reference Document DIA-08-1004-006, Acquisition Threat Support series, dated 6 April 2010.

[The Role of Superconductors in Gravity Research](DIRD_The_Role_of_Superconductors_in_Gravity_Research_2010.pdf) (2010), Defense Intelligence Agency, Defense Intelligence Reference Document DIA-08-1003-013, Acquisition Threat Support series, dated 23 March 2010.

[Warp Drive, Dark Energy, and the Manipulation of Extra Dimensions](DIRD_Warp_Drive_Dark_Energy_and_the_Manipulation_of_Extra_Dimensions_2010.pdf) (2010), Defense Intelligence Agency, Defense Intelligence Reference Document DIA-08-1004-001, Acquisition Threat Support series, dated 2 April 2010.
