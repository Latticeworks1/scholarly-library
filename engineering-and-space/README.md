# Engineering and Space

Spacecraft program history, geotechnical interpretation, and drafting standards.

[The Interplanetary Pioneers, Volume II: System Design and Development](1972_Corliss_The_Interplanetary_Pioneers_Volume_II_System_Design_and_Development_NASA_SP-279.pdf) (1972), William R. Corliss, NASA SP-279. The second of three volumes on Pioneers 6, 7, 8, and 9, the four spacecraft that were the first to orbit the Sun systematically at widely separated points in space; this volume covers how the Pioneer system was defined, designed, and developed. The other volumes are the summary (SP-278) and operations and scientific results (SP-280).

[Settle3 CPT Data Interpretation Theory Manual](2025_Rocscience_Settle3_CPT_Data_Interpretation_Theory_Manual.pdf) (2025), Rocscience. Documents the empirical correlations used to turn cone penetration test readings (tip resistance, sleeve friction, pore pressure) into soil parameters such as unit weight, overburden and pre-consolidation stress, soil behaviour type index, shear wave velocity, equivalent SPT N60, hydraulic conductivity, and friction angle, with notes on their reliability.

[MIL-STD-100G: Department of Defense Standard Practice for Engineering Drawings](1997_MIL-STD-100G_DoD_Standard_Practice_for_Engineering_Drawings.pdf) (June 9, 1997). The 84-page US Department of Defense standard for preparing engineering drawings, superseding MIL-STD-100F of September 1996.
