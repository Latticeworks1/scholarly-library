# Catalog

A short summary of each document, in the order of the file names.

[112 Indoors: Improving Emergency Response with Indoor Location and Indoor Maps](112_Indoors_Improving_Emergency_Response_with_Indoor_Location_and_Indoor_Maps_EENA_2026.pdf) (June 2, 2026). Explains that a growing share of calls to 112 come from mobile phones inside buildings, where location is hard to determine, and that even available indoor location data is of limited use in multi-story or complex buildings unless it is accurate.

[2025 UAP Workshop: Narrative Data, Infrastructures, and Analysis](2025_UAP_Workshop_Narrative_Data_Infrastructures_and_Analysis_Workshop_Synthesis_and_Recommendations_AARO_2025.pdf) (2025), All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office, workshop held 5-6 August 2025 at Associated Universities, Inc., report 26-P-0344. Synthesis and recommendations from breakout sessions on identifying, accessing, integrating, cleaning, and analyzing UAP data sources at scale.

[A Civilian Astronomer's Guide to UAP Research](A_Civilian_Astronomers_Guide_to_UAP_Research_Villarroel_Krisciunas_2024.pdf) (2024), Beatriz Villarroel and Kevin Krisciunas, arXiv:2411.02401. A review of how astronomers can test specific hypotheses about UAP, including extraterrestrial visitation, by analogy with calibration and interpretation in astronomical discovery, with a toy model of networked extraterrestrial probes.

[A Common Operating Picture Framework Leveraging Data Fusion and Deep Learning](A_Common_Operating_Picture_Framework_Leveraging_Data_Fusion_and_Deep_Learning_Ortiz_2020.pdf) (2020), Benjamin Ortiz and colleagues, Accenture Federal Services. A platform that extracts information from separate data sources with deep learning, fuses it, and presents detected and geolocated events in one real-time picture with forensic review.

[A History of Magic and Experimental Science, Volume II](A_History_of_Magic_and_Experimental_Science_Volume_II_Twelfth_and_Thirteenth_Centuries_Thorndike_1923.pdf) (1923), Lynn Thorndike, Columbia University Press. Volume II covers the twelfth and thirteenth centuries, from the early scholastics and Adelard of Bath to Petrus Hispanus and Albertus Magnus, treating each author's magic and natural science together. Volume I is not held; this is a later reprint.

[A Response to Critical Evaluation of Studies Alleging Evidence for Technosignatures in the POSS1-E Photographic Plates by Watters et al. (2026)](A_Response_to_Critical_Evaluation_of_Studies_Alleging_Evidence_for_Technosignatures_in_the_POSS1-E_Photographic_Plates_by_Watters_et_al_2026_Villarroel_Streblyanska_Bruehl_Geier_2026.pdf) (2026), Beatriz Villarroel, Alina Streblyanska, Stephen Bruehl, and Stefan Geier, arXiv:2602.15171, MNRAS preprint. Answers the critique of the statistical analyses in Villarroel et al. (2025) and Bruehl and Villarroel (2025), arguing that it conflates object-level validation with ensemble-level statistical inference.

[A Tradecraft Primer: Basic Structured Analytic Techniques](A_Tradecraft_Primer_Basic_Structured_Analytic_Techniques_DIA_Directorate_for_Analysis_2008.pdf) (2008), Directorate for Analysis, Defense Intelligence Agency, first edition, March 2008. A primer on basic structured analytic techniques for intelligence analysis, published under the Analytic Methodologies series.

[AI Models Collapse When Trained on Recursively Generated Data](AI_Models_Collapse_When_Trained_on_Recursively_Generated_Data_Shumailov_2024.pdf) (2024), Ilia Shumailov and colleagues, Nature. Shows that training on model-generated content causes model collapse, in which the tails of the original data distribution disappear, in language models, variational autoencoders, and Gaussian mixture models.

[AI Special Project Final Report: Assessing How AI Can Improve Public Safety](AI_Special_Project_Final_Report_Assessing_How_AI_Can_Improve_Public_Safety_EENA_2024.pdf) (December 5, 2024). Reports pilots run with emergency call centers on language transcription and translation, noise reduction and audio filtering, and hints and recommendations for call-takers, followed by recommendations.

[Al-Tabari on the Prayers to the Planets](Al-Tabari_on_the_Prayers_to_the_Planets_Pingree_1992.pdf) (1992), David Pingree, Bulletin d'etudes orientales 44, pp. 105-117. Examines the chapter of the Ghayat al-hakim (known in Latin as the Picatrix) that describes how the Sabians of Harran petitioned the planets and their spirits, and shows from a Latin translation that only a small part of it derives from al-Tabari, with the rest drawn mainly from other sources.

[Al-'Usur al-Wusta, volume 29](Al-Usur_al-Wusta_Vol_29_Journal_of_Middle_East_Medievalists_2021.pdf) (2021), The Journal of Middle East Medievalists, a complete 392-page issue. Articles include Liana Saif's long preliminary study of the Pseudo-Aristotelian Hermetica (pp. 20-80), Ahmad Al-Jallad on a digraph in the Quranic consonantal text, David Durand-Guedy on a Saljuq Anatolian ribat and its woman patron, and review essays and book reviews on medieval Islamic history, art, and book culture.

[AML Report Card, 2023 Update](AML_Report_Card_Advanced_Mobile_Location_Deployment_in_30_Countries_EENA_2023.pdf) (2023). The third edition of the survey of Advanced Mobile Location, the handset-based technology that sends a caller's position to emergency services. For 30 countries it covers how messages are transmitted and how fast they arrive, roaming, text-to-112, and the effect on emergency services and citizens.

[Amulets and Talismans](Amulets_and_Talismans_Original_Texts_and_Translations_Budge_1961_Edition.pdf), Sir E. A. Wallis Budge (University Books, 1961 edition of the 1930 work, originally titled Amulets and Superstitions). The original texts, with translations and descriptions, of Egyptian, Sumerian, Assyrian, Hebrew, Christian, Gnostic, and Muslim amulets and magical figures, with chapters on the evil eye, the origin of the amulet, the pentagon, the swastika, the cross, the properties of stones and rings, divination, numbers, the Kabbalah, and ancient astrology.

[An AI System to Help Scientists Write Expert-Level Empirical Software](An_AI_System_to_Help_Scientists_Write_Expert-Level_Empirical_Software_Nature_Aygun_2026.pdf) (2026), Eser Aygun and colleagues, Nature. Presents Empirical Research Assistance (ERA), which combines a large language model with tree search to write scientific software that maximizes a quality metric, with results that include software for forecasting COVID-19 hospitalizations. This copy is the unedited accelerated article preview.

[An Enquiry Concerning Human Understanding, and Selections from A Treatise of Human Nature](An_Enquiry_Concerning_Human_Understanding_and_Selections_from_A_Treatise_of_Human_Nature_Hume_1907.pdf) (1907 edition; Enquiry first published 1748), David Hume, Open Court edition (the Cornell University Library copy). The Enquiry includes the section Of Miracles, which argues that testimony for a miracle must be weighed against the uniform experience that the miracle contradicts.

[An Environmental Analysis of Public UAP Sightings and Sky View Potential](An_Environmental_Analysis_of_Public_UAP_Sightings_and_Sky_View_Potential_Medina_Brewer_Kirkpatrick_2023.pdf) (2023), R. M. Medina, S. C. Brewer, and S. M. Kirkpatrick, Scientific Reports. A Bayesian regression of more than 98,000 publicly reported sightings in the conterminous United States from 2001 to 2020 against light pollution, tree canopy, cloud cover, aircraft, and military installations, finding that more sightings are reported where more of the sky is visible.

[Analytical Limits and Inferential Overreach in the Spatial Modeling of UAP Sighting Reports: A Critique of Medina, Brewer & Kirkpatrick (2023)](Analytical_Limits_and_Inferential_Overreach_in_the_Spatial_Modeling_of_UAP_Sighting_Reports_A_Critique_of_Medina_Brewer_and_Kirkpatrick_2023_SCU_Research_Working_Group_2026.pdf) (2026), SCU Research Working Group, Scientific Coalition for UAP Studies, March 2026. Argues from the published quantities that the 100 percent posterior credibility and 99 percent hotspot claims are not supportable, citing overdispersion, uncorrected multiple testing across 3,109 counties, and temporally mismatched covariates.

[Astral High Magic: De Imaginibus of Thabit ibn Qurra](Astral_High_Magic_De_Imaginibus_of_Thabit_ibn_Qurra_Warnock_2012.pdf) (2012), translated by Christopher Warnock, Renaissance Astrology. An English translation of Thābit ibn Qurra's De imaginibus, working from Carmody's Latin edition, on making talismans timed to the stars.

[Australasian Microtektites and Associated Impact Ejecta in the South China Sea and the Middle Pleistocene Supereruption of Toba](Australasian_Microtektites_and_Associated_Impact_Ejecta_in_the_South_China_Sea_and_the_Middle_Pleistocene_Supereruption_of_Toba_Glass_Koeberl_2006.pdf) (2006), Billy Glass and Christian Koeberl, Meteoritics and Planetary Science 41, pp. 305-326. Reports the first shock-metamorphosed rock fragments, containing coesite and stishovite, found in the Australasian microtektite layer.

[Beyond Steering Vector: Flow-based Activation Steering for Inference-Time Intervention](Beyond_Steering_Vector_Flow-based_Activation_Steering_for_Inference-Time_Intervention_Jin_et_al_2026.pdf) (2026), Zehao Jin, Ruixuan Deng, Junran Wang, Xinjie Shen, and Chao Zhang, Georgia Institute of Technology, arXiv:2605.05892. Argues that activation steering methods are outperformed by simple prompting on AxBench because they restrict interventions to fixed, single-step, position-invariant transforms, and proposes a flow-based method, FLAS.

[Boundary Conditions: Evidence, Experience, and Method in UAP Studies](Boundary_Conditions_Evidence_Experience_and_Method_in_UAP_Studies_Limina_Cifone_2026.pdf) (2026), Michael C. Cifone, editorial, Limina: The Journal of UAP Studies 3(1), 5-10. The editor-in-chief's introduction to the issue, on the temptation to secure the object of study first in a field whose topic appears to announce its object in advance.

[Buletini Mujor i Sizmologjise (Monthly Bulletin of Seismology), No. 9](Buletini_Mujor_i_Sizmologjise_Monthly_Bulletin_of_Seismology_No_9_Polytechnic_University_of_Tirana_2020_03.pdf) (March 2020), Polytechnic University of Tirana. In Albanian. A monthly catalog of earthquakes recorded, located, and analyzed by the Albanian seismological network, with station data, parameter quality analysis, and epicenter maps.

[Chippewa Customs](Chippewa_Customs_Densmore_1929.pdf) (1929), Frances Densmore, Smithsonian Institution Bureau of American Ethnology Bulletin 86. Describes Anishinaabe (Chippewa) life as recorded from Minnesota and Wisconsin informants, including instruction of children toward a happy and successful life, dream fasting, petitions for health and long life, and the Midewiwin.

[Closing the Information Gap in Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP) Studies](Closing_the_Information_Gap_in_Unidentified_Anomalous_Phenomena_UAP_Studies_Stahlman_2024.pdf) (2024), Gretchen R. Stahlman, Florida State University. A scoping review of the scholarly UAP literature from 1967 to 2023 that finds library and information science unrepresented and describes what data curation and information behavior research could contribute.

[Contemplating Eternity: On Time, Death, and Timelessness in Ancient India](Contemplating_Eternity_On_Time_Death_and_Timelessness_in_Ancient_India_Harvard_Dissertation_Yanchevskaya_2019.pdf) (2019), Nataliya Yanchevskaya, Harvard doctoral dissertation. Follows concepts and myths of time from the Rgveda through the Upanisads, the epics, and the philosophical schools, arguing that even the earliest texts distinguish an eternal time from a transitory, profane one, and that time is treated as lord of the universe.

[Critical Evaluation of Studies Alleging Evidence for Technosignatures in the POSS1-E Photographic Plates](Critical_Evaluation_of_Studies_Alleging_Evidence_for_Technosignatures_in_the_POSS1-E_Photographic_Plates_PASA_Watters_Domine_Little_Pratt_Knuth_Szenher_2026.pdf) (2026), Wesley A. [Dynamic Behaviour of Dry and Water-Saturated Sand under Planar Shock Conditions](Dynamic_behaviour_of_dry_and_water-saturated_sand_under_planar_shock_conditions_IJIE_Arlery_Gardou_Fleureau_Mariotti_2010.pdf) (2010), M. [Echoes of Niranj Magic in the Work of Albert the Great](Echoes_of_Niranj_Magic_in_the_Work_of_Albert_the_Great_Rinotas_2024.pdf) (2024), Athanasios Rinotas, Mediterranea 9, pp. 271-292. [Effect of Target Properties and Impact Velocity on Ejection Dynamics and Ejecta Deposition](Effect_of_Target_Properties_and_Impact_Velocity_on_Ejection_Dynamics_and_Ejecta_Deposition_Luther_Zhu_Collins_Wunnemann_2018.pdf) (2018), Robert Luther, Meng-Hua Zhu, Gareth Collins, and Kai Wünnemann, Meteoritics & Planetary Science 53(8). A numerical parameter study of impacts that varies friction, porosity, cohesion and impact velocity. Target strength has the strongest effect on the distribution of ejecta, while the thickness of ejecta deposits as a function of distance is similar across porosities and for impact velocities above about 6 km/s.
